G.ai Token Tray style sheet

"Quiet instrument." A precise, light Mac utility. Hierarchy comes from size and tone, not weight. One tint, system colours, native Liquid Glass, an 8-point rhythm. Opens in light or dark to match your Mac.

Principles

Five rules every screen follows.

Light, not loudRegular and medium weights only. Bold is never used. Big numbers are light.
One tintCandy pink (shared with Gautham's Clipboard) marks what you can press. Green, amber and red appear only for status.
Tone before colourPrimary, secondary and tertiary grey carry the hierarchy. Colour is rare, so it means something.
8-point rhythmEvery gap is 4, 8, 12, 16 or 20. Rows are 40 high. No orphan space, no crowding.
Native materialSystem Liquid Glass and semantic colours, so dark mode and Increase Contrast just work.
